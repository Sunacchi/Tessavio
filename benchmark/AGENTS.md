# AI benchmark instructions

- This area becomes active in Phase C; do not choose models by impression.
- Dataset targets: Italian parsing, multi-intent, ambiguous dates, shifts, images/screenshots, planner cases and edge cases.
- Track schema validity, exact action, date/time accuracy, false action rate, multi-intent recall, latency, cost and fallback rate.
- Optimize extraction for accuracy over eloquence; planner for constraint compliance/usefulness; vision for extraction precision.
- Promote model/prompt/schema changes only after benchmark and controlled canary. Keep fixtures synthetic or consented and free of secrets.
